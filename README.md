# curriculum-k-4
Curriculum Overview: Innovation &amp; Imagination Lab (Grades K–4)This hands-on enrichment curriculum empowers young learners to explore how the natural world inspires modern technology. By blending physical making with digital discovery, students transition from passive consumers of technology to active creators, problem-solvers, and storytellers.

## Core Pillars

### Biomimicry &amp; Micro-Robotics
**Focus:** Observing nature's designs to solve engineering challenges.
**Activities:** Using scurrying micro-robots (like Hexbugs) and tactile building blocks to test how organisms navigate complex environments, translating biological traits into mechanical solutions.

### Animatronics &amp; Physical Computing
**Focus:** Breathing life into inanimate objects through mechanics and electronics.
**Activities:** Designing and programming interactive companion figures, exploring simple circuits, and understanding how movement and sound convey emotion and intent.

### AI &amp; Computer Simulation
**Focus:** Demystifying artificial intelligence and digital worlds through play.
**Activities:** Age-appropriate logic puzzles, pattern recognition games, and interactive simulations that let students see how computers "think," learn, and model real-world systems.

### Technology-Enhanced Literacy
**Focus:** Bridging technical creation with narrative expression.
**Activities:** Writing character backstories for robotic creations, documenting engineering journals, and using digital and physical storytelling to articulate how technology can improve human potential.
